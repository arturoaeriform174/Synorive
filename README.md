## 🛠️ Tech Behind the Magic (Simplified)

Synorive combines three powerful methods:

1.  **Keyword Matching:** Like a classic search engine, it quickly finds exact words.
2.  **Vector Search:** This is the "AI brain." It understands the *meaning* of your query and matches it to concepts, not just exact phrases.
3.  **Reranking:** The brain then re-orders results to put the most relevant items at the top.

All of this happens entirely on your device using a lightweight, efficient engine that respects your privacy by design.
